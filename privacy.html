Privacy policy

Momentum — personal habits, tasks, money and journal tracker · Effective 2 October 2026

In short: your data stays on your device unless you turn on a feature that syncs or sends it. Momentum shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares your data.

What Momentum stores

The information you enter: habits and their history, tasks, money transactions, accounts and budgets, journal entries and journal photos, mood, sleep, workouts, and your settings. By default all of it is stored only on your device.

Optional features that move data off your device

FeatureWhat is sentWhere
Cloud sync (sign in with email or Continue with Google) Your Momentum records, so they are the same on your devices; your sign-in email Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), in an area only your account can read
Journal photos on Google Drive The photos you add to journal entries A hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (Google's drive.appdata permission). Momentum cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
AI features (only if you add your own API key) The text or receipt image you ask about, when you use an AI feature The AI provider you choose: Google Gemini, Groq, Mistral or xAI, under that provider's terms
Backups you create A file with your data (and journal photos) Wherever you save or share it

Google sign-in and Google Drive

With Continue with Google, Momentum receives your Google account's email address and a sign-in token used only to sign you in to Cloud sync. Google Drive access is limited to Momentum's own hidden folder and is used only to back up and sync your journal photos. Momentum's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else.

What Momentum does not do

  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or profiling.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data.
  • Receipt images used for scanning are not kept after the details are extracted.
  • Reminders and notifications are created on your device.

Your control and deletion

  • Edit or delete any entry in the app at any time.
  • Turn off Cloud sync or Google Drive photo sync in Settings › Cloud sync.
  • Remove Momentum's hidden Drive data in Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps › Momentum › Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove Momentum's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete your Cloud sync account and its data, contact us at the address below.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Cloud sync data can only be read by your signed-in account. The optional Vault encrypts its entries with a key derived from your PIN.

Children

Momentum is not directed at children under 13.
